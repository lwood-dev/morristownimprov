The Workshop

Great improvisers make everything look effortless on stage. And they make everyone around them look great as well. In this class, you'll hone your skillsets to create a wide variety of characters that look and sound nothing like you. Gain more confidence starting scenes and get out of your head so you can relax, react in the moment and have fun.

You'll work on

  • Building characters that look and sound nothing like you
  • Starting scenes with confidence
  • Getting out of your head — relaxing and reacting in the moment
  • Making your scene partners look great

Your Instructor

Jeff Kramer

Founder & Artistic Director, ComedySportz San Jose

Jeff Kramer has been a professional improviser at theaters around the US, as well as touring military bases in Italy, Spain, Greece and Turkey as an entertainer. He founded and is the Artistic Director for ComedySportz San Jose, the longest-running show in Silicon Valley. In addition, he has developed and led Applied Improv training programs for such clients as Apple, Airbnb, eBay, Facebook/Meta, Google, and Uber. Jeff was also an adjunct faculty member at Ohlone College where he taught improv for 20 years.
