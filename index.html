      About Us

      It started in a living room.

      How We Started

      Morristown Improv began as a simple idea: get people in the Morristown/Morris Plains area together to play improv games, have fun, and support each other — no auditions, no pressure, no judgment. It started in Liam's living room, after he posted on Reddit about getting together to try improv in a low-stakes, supportive way.

      As more people joined, the meet-ups moved through living rooms, backyards, and finished basements across the community, before we eventually partnered with Morris Plains Jazzercise as our practice space. In 2026, we added a social hour after each meet-up, moving the conversation to a local restaurant.

      What started as a small hangout has grown into a real community — but the mission hasn't changed: helping people connect with improv without the commitment or expense of multi-week classes, and keeping things feeling like we're welcoming you into a comfy, safe living room to practice and play.

      How We've Grown

      From a living room to a full season.

      A few more chapters since that first Reddit post.

      2025

      The Jam takes the stage.

      In February 2025, Morristown Improv Board Member, Karl launched the Improv Jam as an occasional stage show — a handful of nights that year to test whether the Meet-Up's energy could hold a real audience. It did. In February 2026 we moved the Jam to a monthly show, and it's been growing since — because of folks like YOU coming out to play and perform in front of live audiences!

      2026
      January

      Deeper community connections with sponsors.

      In 2026 we built a sponsorship program around the Jam and the Meet-Up — real local businesses who show up, get introduced to our community, and back the room in return. It's less a transaction than a relationship; sponsors become part of the crew, not just a logo on a page. See our sponsors →

      June

      Share the Laughs.

      Morristown Improv Board Member, Marcus pitched, launched, and now produces Share the Laughs — free monthly improv shows for senior centers and retirement communities. They're not open to the public, but they're one of the things we're proudest of. See how to bring us to yours →

      Who It's For

      Total beginners. People who used to do theater in high school. People who just want to meet new people. People building professional presentation and presence skills. People who've done improv for years. Everyone plays at their own pace — nobody's forced on stage.

      First-timers Former theater kids New to the area Building confidence Improv veterans

      We believe improv changes people for the better — but it's the act of creating and sharing, live and in person, that's the real magic. That human connection is the heartbeat of everything we do.

      Team

      The people behind the play.

      Morristown Improv is run entirely by volunteers who show up because they love it.

      Liam Wood

      Founder
      Read Liam's bio

      Liam first fell for improv watching Whose Line Is It Anyway as a kid, and ever since has thought of great improv as a kind of magic. When he looked around for opportunities to do improv in Morristown, he found none, so he decided to make his own. The rest is history. Morristown Improv fulfills Liam's love of teaching, learning, and creating. He continues to be amazed and grateful every day for what has grown out of a single reddit post and the hope that other people in town might want to play some improv games together.

      Karl Ring

      Board Member · Improv Jam Producer
      Read Karl's bio

      Karl loves to play — whether hosting the Jam or jumping in with community members at Meet-Ups, the Jam, or Share the Laughs shows, creating and storytelling with others is his North Star. He trained formally in long- and short-form improv at Improv Boston, Second City LA, iO West, and specialized workshops, performing across the country along the way. Karl joined Morristown Improv in 2023 — one of five people who showed up at Liam's apartment on a rainy Saturday to play — and went on to launch the Improv Jam in February 2025. He also loves coaching, and helps lead warm-ups and chat 1:1 with members at events.

      Marcus Ippolito

      Board Member · Share the Laughs Producer
      Read Marcus's bio

      Marcus is an improviser, performer, and storyteller who believes the best comedy happens when people stop worrying about being perfect and fully commit to the moment. In 2024 he was part of the first graduating class of The Second City Brooklyn's improv curriculum and has been headfirst into the improv world since. He performs with Too Old For That!, Morristown Improv, and Jersey Devil Comedy out of Cape May, and continues sharpening his craft with groups including Vivid Stage, Howdy Stranger, Shore Thing Theater, The Magnet Theater, and UCB. Marcus pitched, launched, and now produces Share the Laughs, MI's free community give-back series.
